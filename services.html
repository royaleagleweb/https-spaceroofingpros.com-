Our services

Complete Roofing Solutions in South Florida

Whether you need a quick repair or a full re-roof, we deliver durable craftsmanship and a clean, professional experience.

01 · Asphalt Shingles

Architectural Asphalt Shingle Roofing

The most popular roofing material in America, modern architectural shingles offer outstanding durability, curb appeal, and value. We install premium lines from GAF Timberline, Owens Corning Duration, and CertainTeed Landmark — all backed by manufacturer warranties up to 50 years.

  • Algae-resistant, Class A fire-rated
  • Wind warranties up to 150 mph (HVHZ)
  • Synthetic underlayment + ice & water shield
  • Full ridge ventilation system
02 · Tile

Clay & Concrete Tile Roofing

Barrel and S-tile roofs define South Florida’s skyline — and a properly installed tile roof can last 50 to 75 years. We install new systems and re-felt or re-batten existing roofs to extend their life without losing the original Mediterranean character.

  • Boral, Eagle, and US Tile profiles
  • Re-felt & re-deck preserving original tile
  • Bird-stop, ridge, and rake detailing
  • Hurricane-rated fastening & foam-set systems
03 · Metal

Standing Seam & Stone-Coated Metal

Metal roofing combines a sleek modern profile with extraordinary longevity. Standing seam, corrugated, and stone-coated aluminum systems handle Florida sun, salt air, and hurricane winds while reflecting heat to keep attic spaces cool.

  • Kynar 500 finishes — 30+ year color warranty
  • Concealed-fastener standing seam
  • Stone-coated steel that mimics tile or shake
  • Energy Star-rated reflective coatings
04 · Cool Roofs

HVHZ-Compliant Cool Roof Systems

South Florida’s High-Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ) code demands reflective, wind-rated roofing. Our Miami-Dade NOA-approved cool roofs earn FPL energy rebates and PACE financing while dramatically lowering summer cooling costs.

  • Miami-Dade NOA-approved membranes
  • Single-ply TPO and PVC systems
  • Elastomeric coatings & restorations
  • Rebate paperwork handled for you
05 · Flat & Torch Down

Modified Bitumen, TPO & Built-Up Roofing

Flat and low-slope roofs need specialized installation. Our commercial-grade systems are built for ponding, foot traffic, and decades of UV exposure — common on mid-century homes, condo towers, and commercial properties.

  • 2-ply & 3-ply modified bitumen
  • Cold-applied & torch-applied options
  • Mechanically-attached and fully-adhered TPO
  • Drain, scupper, and parapet detailing
06 · Repairs & Maintenance

Roof Repairs, Tune-Ups & Emergency Service

Not every roof needs replacing. Our tune-up program addresses the small issues — flashing, cracked tiles, worn boots, ventilation — that cause big leaks down the road. After major storms, we offer same-day emergency tarping.

  • Leak detection with photo report
  • Flashing, valley, and skylight repair
  • Tile and shingle replacement
  • 24/7 emergency tarp & dry-in
