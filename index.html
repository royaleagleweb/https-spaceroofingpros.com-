FAQ’s

How do I know if my roof needs repair or replacement?

Signs like missing or curled shingles, sagging, or visible interior leaks indicate it’s time for a professional inspection. Our experts can evaluate your roof and recommend the best solution for long-term protection.

What types of roofing materials do you work with?

We install asphalt shingles, clay and concrete tile, standing-seam and stone-coated metal, TPO, and modified-bitumen flat-roof systems — every option matched to your home and budget.

Can you handle emergency roofing repairs?

Yes — we offer 24/7 emergency tarp and dry-in service across Miami-Dade, Broward, and Palm Beach. Most calls receive on-site response the same day.

Do you provide warranties on your work?

Every replacement comes with our 25-year workmanship warranty plus full manufacturer coverage on materials. Repairs include a transferable craftsmanship guarantee.

How do I schedule a roof inspection?

Call (305) 555-1234 or use the form above. We typically schedule inspections within 48 hours, free of charge.
