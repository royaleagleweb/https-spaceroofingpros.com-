Our story

Started in a garage. Built on referrals.

Space Roofing Pros began in 2010 when our founder — a third-generation roofer — set out to bring transparent pricing and old-world craftsmanship back to South Florida roofing. What started with one truck and a handful of friends has grown into a 30-person team serving thousands of homeowners across Miami-Dade, Broward, and Palm Beach.

Most of our work still comes from the same place: word-of-mouth from neighbors who trust the way we treat their homes. We never use day labor, never leave debris behind, and never charge for things we didn’t do. That simple commitment is why we’re here today.
